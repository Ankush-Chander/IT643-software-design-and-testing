# Proposal 3: The Verdict

**Q1 — Zero strategic contribution.** Students already use WhatsApp, Instagram, Moodle. Adds nothing to teaching, research, or administration — near-zero adoption after launch week.

**Q2 — Impossible team math.** Competing with thousand-person product teams using students who graduate every year. Maintenance dies with the founding batch.

**Q3 — Integration nightmare.** Payments need bank/UPI compliance; LMS must sync with academic records. Each integration alone is a full project.
